A Cause I Believe In

keepandroidopen.org

Android was built on the promise of freedom — and that promise still matters. Android was born from an open-source vision: the idea that the device in your pocket should belong to you. Not to a corporation, not to a walled garden, but to the person holding it. The freedom to tinker, to customize, to repair, and to choose — these aren't just features. They're principles.

As ecosystems close and choices shrink, I believe it's on us — the users, the tinkerers, the curious — to defend that openness. Whether it's installing a custom ROM, rooting with KernelSU, or simply advocating for the right to repair, every small act keeps the spirit of Android alive.
